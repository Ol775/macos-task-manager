# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, give and accept feedback graciously, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's private information, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option or contact [@Ol775](https://github.com/Ol775) through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Ol775/macos-task-manager/security/advisories/new) if it is sensitive.
